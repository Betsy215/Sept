for documentation for future agents"), on main after 2026.11.24 (build 1) went live. Five passes: money/saves/ads,
day gameplay, screen flow/UI, visuals + docs drift, market research. Nothing was changed by the review itself.

"Confirmed" = the code path was followed by the reviewer and, where marked (checked), re-read by me.

(`OrderSystem.ExpireOrder`), so a 0-star day can earn more. Days 2-3 are much harder than day 1: items per order

## Worth knowing (surprises for a newcomer)
1. Unity IAP starts only in the Shop scene (`RemoveAds.Init()` from `ShopManager`). Remove Ads ownership is the
   PlayerPrefs flag `ftc_ads_removed`; after a reinstall it is rebuilt from the receipt only when a shop opens. Safe
   today because ads start on day 3; moving ads earlier would break that.
2. Play erases the save with one tap (no confirm since 9 Oct). A new game keeps `starsBought` and Remove Ads.
3. Coins reach the wallet order by order (`AddScoreImmediately`); Restart rolls back to `scoreAtLevelStart`
   (memory only). Daily Treats coins wait in `ftc.daily.pending` until Continue/New game.
4. A card whose `itemName` or GameObject name starts with "Star" goes to the star tab and is paid in stars
   (`ShopManager.TabOf`); runtime cards are cloned from the Candies card as `ItemType.Decor`; the patience bonus counts
   every `purchasedDecor` entry, so only the two pots may go through `PurchaseDecor`.
5. Every money/state change re-serialises the whole session and calls `PlayerPrefs.Save()`; the wallet is a float
   (whole cents stop being exact around $65k-130k).
6. Stars come from the hen's `starStates` (`StarProgressBar.UpdateStars`), exact only after `CloseDay()`.
7. `OrderSystem.OnEnable` rebuilds counters and the food list; `GamePhaseManager` toggles the order system around the
   arrangement, so the list is built from the table at play start.
8. Kitchen state lives in statics/persistent objects (`KitchenTimerHelper`, `CooldownRegistry`,
   `OvenKitchenBase.Recipes`, `KitchenFlow`, `AppleInKitchen.Count`); new kitchen state must be cleared in
   `KitchenDay.End` (`Kitchen/KitchenClock.cs:26-36`).
9. Notifications package 2.3.2 reads `ProjectSettings/NotificationsSettings.asset`; the copy under
   `Assets/Editor/com.unity.mobile.notifications` is stale.
10. Long one-line UI builders with trailing `//` comments have twice swallowed code (R1, and an alpha line in
    `TutorialDirector` earlier). After editing such a line, grep for code-like text after `//`.
11. `MapScreen.CameFromDay` is never set true; the `if (CameFromDay)` branch in `PlayDay` is dead.
12. All player-facing text is hard-coded English in runtime UI builders; no localisation layer. Scenes are 1080x1920,
    match 0.5; the map and update card use Expand.
13. The served/lost count of a day shows only on TestFlight (test-build receipt line); store analytics has none.

## Market research summary (full sources in the review session; key ones below)
Situation: ~50 installs/week, mostly Philippines/Indonesia/Vietnam, 0 ratings, ads $0.04-0.70/day (normal for
~20-40 DAU in SEA: SEA iOS eCPMs are a fraction of the US), most players leave in days 1-3.
Already in the game: Apple rating prompt once, at the shop after day 3 (`Utils/RatingPrompt.cs`) — but most players
never reach day 3; first between-days ad at the start of day 3 (`MapScreen.FirstAdDay = 2`).

Ideas, quick wins first (owner decides; store changes need their yes each time):
1. Ask for the rating earlier: after the first 3-star day (Apple: after a completed task, never during onboarding).
2. Fewer early ads: first interstitial later (research: day 5 or second session), and never right after a 1-2 star day.
3. Gentler days 2-3 (patience or items per order; the Kid's max order), so new players keep 3 stars.
4. Winter chapter: Featuring Nomination in App Store Connect now (needs 2+ weeks) and an In-App Event ("New Season")
   uploaded ~2 weeks before launch, with 1920x1080 and 1080x1920 art without text.
5. ASO: "cooking game"/"bakery" in the subtitle, all 100 keyword characters used.
6. Lower prices for the Philippines/Indonesia/Vietnam; a cheap starter pack after day 3; Remove Ads + bonus bundle.
7. Rewarded "double coins" at day end, and "retry with extra time" after a weak day.
8. Ad mediation (Unity LevelPlay + AdMob) for better fill in Asia.
9. Store page localisation (Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai) before in-game text.
10. Short videos (YouTube Shorts first), a rules-following Reddit post and a press email at the winter launch.
Benchmarks: casual D1 median ~22 % (top quartile ~30 %), D7 ~4 % (top quartile 6-7 %).
