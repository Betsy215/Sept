1Sale prices

The sale price of each item

  • Chapter 1: coffee $10, bread $15, apple $20, juice $20, cookies $30, strawberry cake $35 a slice, choux $35.
  • Chapter 2: coconut $30, ice cream $15 per scoop (a three-scoop glass is $45).
  • Plus a tip: price × seconds left on the order × 10 % (some customers tip more). A $30 order served with 6 seconds left pays $18 in tips.
  • A replayed day pays only part of this (unchanged).

2Kitchen times

As of test build 2026.11.23 (2).

The time for each item after the kitchen redesign

  • Bread: 5 s in the oven.
  • Cookies: 3 s in the oven.
  • Choux: about 9 s from the oven tap to the table: oven 3 s, mixer 2.5 s, fruit board 2 s, shown finished 0.9 s, sparkles 0.6 s. No taps after the oven.
  • Strawberry cake: the same, about 9 s.
  • Choux and cake share the mixer and the board: if both are cooking, the second waits its turn (up to 2.5 s more).
  • Apples and coconuts: back 8 s after the last one is picked.
  • Ice cream blender: 3 s with four flavours, 1.8 s with two.
  • Waffle machine: about 2.4 s.
  • Coffee bags, juice: at once.
  • With the Kitchen upgrade (15 stars) all of it is a quarter shorter: bread 3.8 s, cookies 2.3 s, choux and cake about 7 s.

3Days and chapters

  • Chapter 1: 12 days. Chapter 2: 16 days. 28 in all.
  • Day numbers restart at 1 in every chapter for the player (map, day card, play, day-end card, journal stamps). The save keeps one running number: chapter 2's first day is the 13th. The analytics use the running number.
  • Chapter 2 opens when day 9 of chapter 1 is finished and its big star on the map is tapped: one extra star, the truck drives to the arrow. The star waits for anyone who has finished day 9 and not taken it; the map opens on chapter 1 while it waits. Days 10 to 12 stay playable.
  • Chapter 2's first day: a tutorial (waffle machine, blender, ice cream) with the gift of a fourth patience flower; every ice cream that day is a waffle bowl with one or two scoops.
  • Patience: three flowers in chapter 1, four in chapter 2, each lasting as long (a third more time in chapter 2).
  • Thinking: a customer thinks 2 s (three dots) before the order shows; nothing can be served meanwhile and patience starts after.
  • Patience and serving: every served item fills the customer's patience up to the next whole flower.
  • Tip levels (shown on the receipt; the money is not changed): by the flowers left when the order is finished. Perfect = all (chapter 2: three or four), green with a PERFECT! tag; Nice = two, yellow with a NICE! tag; Just OK = one, grey. The perfect sound goes with Perfect.
  • Stars for a day: 3 = every customer served whole, 2 = 90 % (rounded), 1 = more than half.
  • A day's end, restart or leaving stops the whole kitchen: ovens, mixer and board are cleared, trees are full again.
  • Map: when a new day has been finished the truck drives along the line to the next day, then the day card opens.

4Update card

  • Once per start the game asks the App Store which version is out (it sends only our app's number).
  • If the store's version is newer than the copy and has been out at least 36 hours, a card with one UPDATE button covers the screen; it opens our store page. No way round it.
  • No network or no clear answer: no card. Never over a running day (it waits for the start menu, map or shop).
  • In every copy from release 2026.11.23 on; older copies have no check.

5Customers per day

How a day fills (24 November). A customer comes every few seconds while the day's clock runs, at most two waiting at once; a new one only comes when a place is free. So a quick player meets the most customers; a slow one fewer. Each customer orders a few items.
